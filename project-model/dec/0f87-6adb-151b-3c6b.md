# Pan and zoom keep the view within the current layer's extent (zoomed out no further than the view fits, centre clamped in the display system); a fit to content the layer's extent does not hold switches to the default world-wide layer; the display system is always one of the layer's choices, else Web Mercator

<!-- rationale, detail, alternatives considered -->

Refined 2026-10-23 (user report: in UTM 33N, Bergen could not be brought
on screen): the extent's edges are curved in a national system, so each
side of the view stops at the extent's edge where the view meets it -- the
west and east edges across the view's rows, the south and north edges across
its columns -- rather than at the rectangle inscribed in the whole extent.
Zooming out stops when the view is as wide as the extent at its widest, so
the whole extent fits on screen (with what the curvature leaves around its
corners). In Web Mercator, straight edges: the view stays in the rectangle.
