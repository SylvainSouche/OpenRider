# Method: sparse ~5 m grid; each track marks cells within ~8 m of its path, counting once per cell; corridor = cells crossed by >= 2 distinct tracks; skeleton (thinning) traced junction to junction into a segment graph; tracks snapped to the nearest segment within tolerance; mean track = average of the matched tracks along each segment. Parameters: cell size, tolerance, min tracks. Known risk: trails closer than ~15 m merge. Reference: KDE-based map inference (Biagioni & Eriksson 2012)
