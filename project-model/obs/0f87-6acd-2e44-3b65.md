# MacPorts clang 19 and 22 (not Apple clang, not gcc 15): the macOS SDK's math.h partially includes float.h (#define __need_infinity_nan) and libc++'s float.h wrapper guard then blocks the full float.h, so FLT_MIN/FLT_MAX or INFINITY/NAN are undefined depending on include order; hits Dear ImGui and ImPlot

Supersedes 0f87-6acd-2689-e92a (MacPorts clang/libc++ 22.1.8: <mutex> includes a partial <float.h> (infinity/NaN only) whose guard hides FLT_MIN/FLT_MAX from later includes; workaround: include imgui.h (or <float.h>) before standard headers).

Carries forward what remained valid and states what changed.
